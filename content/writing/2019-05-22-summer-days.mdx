The summer used to be mine,\
to be spent however I pleased.\
I would do nothing, and I was nothing.\
I was a buoy, floating on the seas.

Now I cannot remember the summer days.\
I think I still feel these days from within\
I stand under the same sun.\
The same soil is beneath my feet.

Yet the me that would laugh\
and get sunburned every single day\
has moved on from the summer.\
I am now in the fall or maybe winter.

Expecting something to come\
though I’m not sure what that is.\
Summer days seem so far away\
And my childhood with it.

The beauty of summer is best spent\
by the soul that has a summer inside.\
Though I may be at peace and happy,\
I never quite reach the summer that I was as a child.
